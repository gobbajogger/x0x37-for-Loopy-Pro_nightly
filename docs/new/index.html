Release status

Updated 13 November 2026. This page describes the NIGHTLY channel, not a production release.

0.0.26 — hardware-test candidate 94197fc8

A replacement candidate is available for owner-operated testing, built from exact source 94197fc839b00aff47b98ca02f3c6396e4a6eabf. It includes the reviewed boot-guard, model-identity and sample-integrity corrections. Elite and Pro builds and package checks pass; free flash is 8,440 / 8,448 bytes and free pool is 8,340 bytes each. Physical startup, restart and recovery are not yet verified. This is not a stable-release recommendation.

Original 0.0.26 (600b3b45) — withdrawn

Removed from the installer after reports of updates stuck at Restarting and very long or failed startup. The original 600b3b45 packages remain withdrawn. Do not reinstall those saved packages as a recovery step.

The old direct download URLs have been removed. Development history remains for investigation.

Withdrawal record

0.0.25.1 — available development package

0.0.25.1 remains available as the known-good fallback reported by the owner. Its on-device version label is 0.0.25. Model-specific packages, exact source and verification details are available in the installer. A successful recovery on one device is not proof of validation for every model.

Installer restart handling

The installer now handles repeated final loader acknowledgements and bounds reconnect attempts. These changes do not establish the cause of the firmware startup failure.
