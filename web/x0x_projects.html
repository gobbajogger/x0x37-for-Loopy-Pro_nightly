On the keyboard

Load replaces the live project. Reset clears the song, patterns, sounds and project settings; samples stay. Reset turns autosave on and may overwrite the saved project. Export first to keep your work.

Details: reset & recovery

Reset also clears motion and stops playback and recording; device role settings stay in place. Arrangement-only clear is unavailable in this browser. To clear only the arrangement, use SONG → CLEAR SONG on the keyboard, keeping patterns, sounds, settings and samples.

Keep USB connected during transfers. After a sample-transfer failure, the page attempts to restore the previous samples and reports the result. Keep the recovery file for reconnecting.
