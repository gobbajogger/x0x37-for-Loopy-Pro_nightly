# Product-demo research used for this revision

Primary pages read on 2026-11-14:

- Apple iPad Pro: https://www.apple.com/ipad-pro/ — concise product promise; highlights before closer inspection; deeper explanations tied to visible app use.
- Teenage Engineering OP-XY: https://teenage.engineering/products/op-xy — instrument/control/display relationships, musical examples and deeper specifications separated.
- OP-XY how-to: https://teenage.engineering/guides/op-xy/how-to — a named physical action, resulting state and return to music.

Applied only as principles: one main exploration action, real recorded firmware as the visual centerpiece, a few musical outcomes, short guides and progressive disclosure. No Apple or Teenage Engineering copy, branding, imagery, CSS or product behavior was copied. DnBOX retains its charcoal/lime identity and original prose.

Browser scope is exact in evidence/inspiration-browser.json. The TE guide's Back to index was exercised successfully. Apple's hydrated Learn how M5 powers performance control was clicked and Escape was sent, but no dialog or close control was observed, so this does not claim a tested successful closer-look animation/modal. Initial proxy-certificate and duplicate-link selector errors were diagnosed; the latter was fixed by selecting the first identical index link. These public-page checks are research only, unrelated to DnBOX browser acceptance.
