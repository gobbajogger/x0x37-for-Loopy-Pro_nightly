## User promise and information order

Someone may reach DnBOX without owning an SMK-37 or knowing what a groovebox does. The site must answer in this order: **what DnBOX makes possible**, **which instrument/version it applies to**, **how to hear or make one first pattern**, **what else connects**, and **how to install/recover without losing existing work**. The concrete source-backed story is: a small SMK-37 Elite/Pro can sequence 909/808 drums, Chicago/Miami bass, and personal BREAK loops, store a project, and be played without a DAW. External USB and optional Bluetooth/SMC expand control **only with visible capability and qualification boundaries**. Juno/Drone belong to the 0.0.26 development candidate, **not** 0.0.25.1. Do not claim music has “never been easier” as a comparative fact, universal phone compatibility, real keyboard boot from an emulator image, or zero user mistakes.

**Interaction rule:** experimentation and happy accidents stay easy; consequential actions clearly reveal the object at risk, predicted change, cancel/undo/backup when actually available, and truthful completion. Learning should reward curiosity, not require reading incident logs. Product hero: one primary **See how a beat starts** task, a subordinate **Install / update** route for owners, and persistent **Recover** access; no fake playable-demo button. Exact firmware/build evidence lives in expandable details or the release page. Example information order is a research proposal **not owner-approved copy**.

**Proof vocabulary:** S25 = published 0.0.25.1 source `79280e76`; S26 = 0.0.26 candidate source `94197fc8`; D26 = SMK-specific [#884 manual](https://github.com/gobbajogger/X0X37-for-Loopy-Pro/pull/884) at `97a88b44` (docs PR, not published/approved firmware UI); P890 = inert preview at `4b9c4e91`; LM = published main `gh-pages` source `cd345c94` and installer blob `fe3350b2`; LN = nightly `gh-pages` `5734c215` and installer blob `265bad08`; R906 = [ten source-qualified musician findings](https://github.com/gobbajogger/X0X37-for-Loopy-Pro/blob/29caa76690366455abadbf1d17f9943bac901c80/docs/research/ux-musician-workflows-20261113.md). **Code/manual observations are not physical tests.** The owner reported Elite 0.0.25.1/0.0.26 installations and a 0.0.26 restart/boot; **installed hash, Pro device operation and full musical function are not verified**. ## Research forwarded to firmware UX (#891), not a competing implementation

Use the evidence IDs in [#906](https://github.com/gobbajogger/X0X37-for-Loopy-Pro/pull/906). These are proposed *audits*, not accepted firmware features:

- **R03/R06/R08:** User task: find/select the *currently sounding* part and edit a step without a mode accident. Adapt stable selection/edit/back language, active-target cues, hold-to-explain; **SMK constraint:** 240×240 LCD, two 8-pad rows, printed legends that mean different things in clip mode, held-note ownership. Preserve fast entry, do not add a verbose overlay that obscures notes.
- **R01/R02:** User task: make an unexpected good variation and return to it. Adapt predictable SAVE/undo scope and named queued/failed save states; **constraint:** source already has HOME+REC undo and HOME+PLAY redo, limited flash/RAM, no proven unlimited histories and live chains are not saved.
- **R04/R06:** User task: cue/cancel the next section while sustaining notes and effects. Adapt visible NEXT/CHN and target ownership rather than more menu layers; **constraint:** real-time audio deadline <11,609 µs/core, zero observed dropouts, no unexpected releases, current UI/part-order semantics.
- **R05:** User task: reach a first kick without a manual. Audit existing 909 SEQ default, BD black 1, white/pad 1/5/9/13, PLAY and the recovery from accidental HOME/clip mode before proposing additions; **constraint:** do not erase expert shortcuts or alter the verified 37-key map.
- **R07:** User task: hold a chord and sweep sound while changing screen. Audit control latency, LCD dirty rectangles, dropout/FX tail, not a prettier CPU number; **constraint:** no slower firmware gate, native memory floors and device-vs-emulator evidence separation.
- **R09/R10:** User task: trust that music survived update/recovery. Adapt consistent data-scope and confirmation vocabulary where relevant in firmware UI; **constraint:** loader-only cannot read an absent app, factory installs have no DnBOX export, protected partitions/bootloader must not be touched.

**Rejected inferences from R906 remain rejected:** competitor anecdotes are not DnBOX bugs; no universal best control grammar, copied artwork, force-flash remedy, unlimited undo, device qualification from emulator, or genre-forcing defaults. YouTube audiovisual playback was NOT_RUN in #906. #891 owner determines which audit finding, if any, needs a firmware change and submits that design for the owner's approval.
