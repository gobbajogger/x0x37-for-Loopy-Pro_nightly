Owner-authorized isolated design prototype; root owns implementation. No deployment, firmware/emulator changes, or stopped-worker restart.

Consumed feedback: v2 repeated cards/headings feel generic; owner said explanation pages “suck dry”; explain physical keybed/pad layouts against actual screen and current mode; more purposeful teaching animation; replace text wherever a clear visual communicates it, preserving accessible equivalents and concise data-loss warnings.

Direction: a spacious instrument-first opening, a recorded listening desk if authentic audio qualifies, a lime rhythm sketch, then a visual four-kick lesson. Different compositions serve invitation, discovery and doing. Secondary v2 preparation/recovery/file paths remain reachable.

Considered: (1) Four places to start — locate real keys/pads, trace mappings, compare a completed capture; (2) Look/touch/notice — annotated recordings, less physical orientation; (3) Listen to an idea — recorded music first, conditional on audio provenance. Choose1 as learning core, borrow2 for labels and3 only for qualified captured playback. This implements the delegated brief without an additional approval gate.

Teaching: separate illustrated hands from unchanged recorded screens. Manual frame changes and opt-in timed playback share exact mappings. Pause/repeat/previous/next, keyboard access, route/background interruption, reduced-motion manual equivalence. Do not animate invented firmware LCD states. Four-kick playback and intermediate edit captures are missing; expected results are labelled expectations rather than performed responses. Actual mode mapping is source-backed; physical pad geometry must be established before diagram inclusion.

Acceptance: inspect desktop1440, tablet768, mobile390 and narrow320; follow tasks and divergent outcomes; test teaching pause/repeat/end/interruption/reduced-motion/keyboard; verify unchanged capture hashes and audio provenance; fresh candid blind review of comprehension and fidelity, then fix and recheck.
